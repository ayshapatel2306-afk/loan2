Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our ordering process and delivery

How far in advance should I place my order?

For standard cakes from our catalog, orders should be placed at least 12-24 hours in advance. For custom theme cakes (multi-tier or complex designs), we recommend ordering 3-5 days in advance so we can prepare specialized details.

Do you offer eggless and sugar-free options?

Yes! We specialize in 100% vegetarian eggless cakes. You can choose the "Eggless" checkbox during custom order form checkouts. We also offer select sugar-free options upon specific request via the special instructions field.

What are your delivery charges and regions?

We charge a flat delivery fee of $4.99 within a 15km radius of our main kitchen. For deliveries further out, incremental charges may apply. We deliver in specialized chilled containers to ensure your cream remains perfectly cold.

Can I cancel or modify my cake order?

Order cancellations or modifications are fully refunded if completed at least 24 hours before the scheduled delivery time. Cancellations inside 24 hours will incur a 50% recovery fee since our bakers would have already started baking your order.
